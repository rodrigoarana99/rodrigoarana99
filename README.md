### About Me

Quantitative Researcher with a background in Actuarial Science, Mathematical Statistics, and Finance. I build mid-frequency trading strategies, derivatives analytics, and automated research infrastructure at Superavit Investment Fund. Based in Buenos Aires.

---

### What I Do

- **Regime Detection** -- Developed ZEROLINE, a physics-inspired (quantum harmonic oscillator) framework for market regime classification and autonomous SPY trading
- **Academic Strategy Replication** -- Replicated and audited 52 published trading papers end-to-end; identified bias issues, alpha decay patterns, and validated production-viable systems
- **Derivatives Analytics** -- Variance swap replication, volatility surface modeling, real-time Greeks computation, term structure analysis
- **Multi-Agent Systems** -- Built a 7-agent LLM-orchestrated pipeline for automated research from paper ingestion to backtesting and execution
- **Factor & Statistical Modeling** -- ICA-based return decomposition, cross-asset factor pricing, interest rate probability models for Fed decision forecasting
